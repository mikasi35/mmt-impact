The programme strategy sets out what MMT Impact intends to deliver across its
first three-year cycle, in what sequence, and against what reporting
commitments.

It is published as a plan and must not be read as a record of delivered results.
Progress against it is reported through the impact dashboard and, from the first
full reporting cycle, through the annual report.
