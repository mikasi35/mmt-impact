The governance and accountability framework sets out how MMT Impact is governed,
who holds which decision rights, and where independent oversight sits.

A summary of the framework and each policy it contains is published on the
transparency page. The signed framework document is provided to institutional
partners on request during due diligence, and will be published here as a
downloadable document once formally adopted by the Board.
