The monitoring, evaluation and learning framework defines what MMT Impact
measures, how each indicator is defined, and the standard of evidence required
before a figure is published.

It also sets out the disaggregation standard applied across all programmes, the
participant feedback and complaints mechanisms that feed into programme
decisions, and the circumstances in which a published figure will be restated.
