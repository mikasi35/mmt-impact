The organisational profile is the document most often requested at the start of a
due diligence process. It is published on this site as a formatted page that can
be printed or saved as a PDF directly from the browser, so that the version a
partner receives is always the current one.

It covers the organisational mandate, governance and committee structure, the six
programme areas, the six-stage delivery method, the institutional capacity
position including the MMT Group relationship, reporting commitments and contact
points.
