MMT Impact has published the governance and accountability framework under which
it operates, ahead of first programme delivery.

The framework sets out the organisation’s board structure and the remits of its
three standing committees: Finance, Audit & Risk; Programme & Safeguarding; and
the Senior Management Team.

## Safeguarding reports directly to the Board

The framework establishes a direct reporting line from the Safeguarding and PSEA
Focal Point to the Board, independent of the executive management chain. The
arrangement is intended to remove the conflict that arises when safeguarding
concerns are escalated only through the line management structure whose conduct
may be in question.

## Policy suite

Twelve policies are set out in the framework, covering safeguarding, child
protection, PSEA, code of conduct, anti-fraud and corruption, whistleblowing,
conflict of interest, procurement, financial management, data protection, health
and safety, and accountability to affected populations.

Summaries of each policy are published on the organisation’s transparency page.
Signed policy documents are available to institutional partners on request during
due diligence, and will be published as downloadable documents as each is
formally adopted.

## Conflict of interest within the wider Group

The conflict of interest policy addresses organisational as well as personal
conflicts, including those that may arise within the wider MMT Group. MMT Impact,
MMT Care and MMT Alliance are separately constituted organisations, and the
framework requires that any engagement between them be declared, registered and
managed on arm’s-length terms.
