MMT Impact has set out the programme priorities that will guide its work in South
Sudan across 2026 to 2028.

The organisation will work across six programme areas: education and skills
development; community support; women and youth empowerment; livelihoods and
economic empowerment; humanitarian response and resilience; and institutional
development and consultancy.

## A defined method, published in advance

Alongside the programme areas, MMT Impact has published the six-stage delivery
method it applies to every programme — listen, partner, design, deliver, measure,
sustain — so that a prospective partner can see how a programme moves from a
community conversation to a documented result, and who is accountable at each
stage.

"Publishing the method before publishing results is deliberate," the organisation
said. "It means we can be held to how we said we would work, not only to the
numbers we report afterwards."

## Reporting commitments

MMT Impact has committed to three reporting practices from the outset:

- Results disaggregated by sex, age and vulnerability, so that strong headline
  figures cannot conceal weak reach among the groups a programme exists to serve.
- Publication of programme targeting methodology alongside results frameworks, so
  the basis on which participants are selected is open to scrutiny.
- Reporting of findings where a programme has underperformed, rather than only
  where it has succeeded.

## Phasing

The 2026 to 2028 period is structured in three phases: establishing the country
office, governance framework and first programme designs during 2026; scaling
delivery and formalising monitoring and evaluation reporting through 2027; and
transitioning mature programmes toward community and institutional ownership from
2028.
