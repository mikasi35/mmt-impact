MMT Impact has opened registration for organisations and individuals seeking to
work with it as partners, suppliers or consultants.

Three registration routes are now available: partnership enquiry, for UN agencies,
governments, NGOs, foundations and private sector organisations; supplier
registration, for organisations providing goods and services; and consultant
registration, for individuals and firms offering technical expertise.

## What registration does and does not mean

Registration places an organisation on MMT Impact’s roster and ensures it is
notified when a relevant opportunity opens. It does not constitute a commitment
to award work, and MMT Impact does not charge any fee for registration.

Where an opportunity is issued, it is published openly on the opportunities page
and awarded through the competitive process set out in the organisation’s
procurement policy.

## Due diligence runs both ways

MMT Impact conducts due diligence on partners, suppliers and contractors,
including safeguarding standards, financial standing and beneficial ownership.

The organisation also publishes its own governance framework, policy suite and
institutional profile so that a prospective partner can conduct the equivalent
assessment before entering a relationship. Partners are encouraged to do so.

## Registering

Registration forms are available on the partnerships pages. Enquiries can also be
directed to partnerships@mmtimpact.org.
