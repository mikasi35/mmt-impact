## Overview

This programme is in design. It applies the pathway model set out in our Women &
Youth Empowerment programme: skills, then the capital or asset required to use
them, then market access, then the mentoring structure that carries an enterprise
through its first difficult year.

## Why it is designed this way

Enterprise training delivered on its own produces very few trading businesses. The
constraint for most participants is not knowledge — it is start-up capital, a route
to a buyer, and the absence of anyone to consult when the first serious problem
arrives in month four.

Each of those is addressed as a designed component rather than assumed away.

## Status

Design is under way, including the market assessment that will determine which
enterprise categories can absorb new entrants without displacing existing traders.
The programme will publish its results framework before delivery begins.
