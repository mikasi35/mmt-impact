## Overview

This programme is planned and not yet funded. It targets the gap between what
smallholder producers grow and what they are able to sell — a gap in which a
substantial share of the value produced is lost to poor storage, weak transport
and fragmented selling.

## Sequencing

Improving yield without improving storage and route to market increases the volume
that spoils. Production, handling and market access are therefore designed and
delivered together, sequenced against the agricultural calendar rather than the
funding cycle.

## Status

Planned. A value chain assessment will precede design, and the programme will not
proceed into delivery in any commodity where the assessment finds the market
cannot absorb additional producers.
