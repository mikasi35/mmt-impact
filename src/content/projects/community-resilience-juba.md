## Overview

This programme is in design. Its central question is not how to deliver support,
but who is currently receiving none — and why.

## Approach

Identification is community-led and community-validated, because targeting done
externally is both less accurate and less trusted. The programme then works on the
two structural gaps that assessment consistently surfaces: community organisations
without the capacity to plan and account for support, and referral pathways that
exist on paper but not in practice.

## Status

Design is under way. The programme will publish its targeting methodology
alongside its results framework, so that the basis on which households are
selected is open to scrutiny.
