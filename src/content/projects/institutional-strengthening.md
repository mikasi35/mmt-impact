## Overview

This facility is in design. It exists because localisation stalls at a systems gap,
not a willingness gap: capable local organisations are excluded from direct funding
by compliance requirements they have never had the resources to build.

## Method

Assessment first, against the standard each organisation actually needs to meet.
Then a sequenced development plan, because attempting everything simultaneously
produces documents nobody uses. Then accompaniment through a real reporting cycle
with the people who will operate the systems.

## Status

In design. The facility will publish its assessment framework so that
participating organisations understand in advance what they are being measured
against.
