## Overview

The Education & Skills Programme in Juba addresses a specific and well-evidenced
gap: young people completing training that does not connect to work. The
programme is built backwards from employer demand rather than forwards from
available curricula.

Before a cohort opens, we establish what local employers, cooperatives and
markets are actually hiring for and what is constraining them. Training is
designed against that finding, and participants are tracked after completion
rather than at graduation.

## Design

Each cohort combines technical instruction with the literacy, numeracy and
work-readiness support that determines whether a technical skill can be used.
Participation barriers are addressed as part of the programme rather than treated
as the participant’s problem to solve — a young woman with childcare
responsibilities and no transport does not attend a course simply because a place
has been offered.

## Reporting

Reach is disaggregated by sex, age and vulnerability from the first cohort, so
that headline completion figures cannot conceal weak reach among the groups the
programme exists to serve. Participant outcomes are tracked at six and twelve
months after completion.
