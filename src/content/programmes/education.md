## Why this programme exists

An education programme is easy to describe and hard to do well. The difficulty is
rarely enrolment — it is retention, quality, and the question of what happens
after. A training centre that graduates a cohort into a labour market with no
demand for their skill has produced certificates, not outcomes.

MMT Impact designs education and skills programming against that test. Before a
curriculum is agreed, we establish what local employers, cooperatives, markets and
public services actually need, what they will pay for, and what is currently
constraining supply. Training is then built to meet that demand, and we track
participants after completion rather than at graduation.

## What delivery looks like

Programmes run through local trainers and, wherever they exist, through existing
schools, training centres and institutions. Where facilities, materials or trainer
capability are the binding constraint, we address that directly rather than
building a parallel structure alongside it.

Every cohort is disaggregated by sex, age and vulnerability from the outset, so
that a programme reporting strong overall numbers cannot conceal the fact that it
is not reaching girls, or displaced learners, or young people with disabilities.
