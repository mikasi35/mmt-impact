## Why this programme exists

Empowerment is among the most used and least examined words in development. Used
loosely, it describes a workshop. Used properly, it describes a measurable shift in
what a person can earn, decide and refuse.

MMT Impact programmes to the second definition. That means we are specific about
what should change for a participant, and we accept being held to it.

## The pathway

Training alone rarely changes anything. A woman who completes a tailoring course
and cannot access a machine, capital, a market or the time to trade has gained a
skill and no income.

So programmes are built as sequences: skills, then the asset or capital required to
use them, then market access or an employer relationship, then the ongoing peer and
mentorship structure that keeps an enterprise trading through its first difficult
year. At each step we identify the barrier that would realistically stop a
participant — childcare, transport, safety, family permission, literacy — and we
address that barrier as part of the programme rather than treating it as someone
else’s problem.

## Measurement

We track income, enterprise survival at twelve months, employment placement and
participation in decision-making structures. These are harder to report than
attendance figures. They are also the only figures that describe whether anything
actually changed.
