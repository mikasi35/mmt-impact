## Why this programme exists

Every community already has a support system. It is made of neighbours, relatives,
elders, women’s groups and faith structures, and it carries almost all of the load.
The question for an external organisation is not whether to build a support system,
but whether it will strengthen the one that exists or quietly compete with it.

MMT Impact works on the first of those. We begin by understanding how support
already moves through a community — who is looked after, by whom, and at what cost
to them — and then work on the specific points where that system is failing.

## Who is missed, and why

The most consistent finding in community assessment is that the people furthest
from support are furthest for structural reasons: distance, disability, stigma,
language, gender, or having no one to advocate for them. General provision does not
reach them. Targeted design does.

That is why disability inclusion is not a separate workstream here. It is a
requirement applied across every programme MMT Impact runs, assessed at design
stage and reported on afterwards.

## Protection

MMT Impact is not a specialist protection agency, and we do not present ourselves
as one. Our role is awareness, safe identification, and referral into the hands of
organisations mandated and equipped to respond — supported by safeguarding and
PSEA obligations that apply to every member of our personnel, every partner and
every contractor.
